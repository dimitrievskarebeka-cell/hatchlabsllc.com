Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

In short: Hatch Labs builds all kinds of apps. We collect as little information as possible, we never sell it, and our apps for children collect no personal information at all.

This policy explains how Hatch Labs LLC ("Hatch Labs", "we", "us") handles information in our apps and on this website. It applies to every app we publish. Where an app works differently, its App Store / Google Play privacy details or its own privacy notice will say so. If you have questions, contact us at hello@hatchlabsllc.com.

Information we collect

What we collect depends on the app. Many of our apps work without an account and collect no personal information. If an app needs information to work (for example, an account, saved data in the cloud or a support request), we will collect only what is needed for that feature, and we will tell you what it is in the app and in its store privacy details.

This may include:

  • Information you give us, such as your email address if you create an account or contact us;
  • App data, such as your settings or content you create, needed for the app to work;
  • Technical information, such as device type, app version and crash reports, used to keep the app working well.

How we use information

  • to provide, maintain and improve our apps;
  • to respond to your questions and support requests;
  • to keep our apps secure and prevent misuse;
  • to meet our legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with others for their own marketing.

Service providers

Some apps may use trusted service providers (for example, for hosting, payments or crash reporting) that process information on our behalf and only under our instructions. Any such services are listed in that app's store privacy details.

Information stored on your device

Many of our apps save progress and settings only on your device. This information is not sent to us, and deleting the app deletes it.

Children's privacy

Some of our apps are made for children, including children under 13. Our children's apps are designed to collect no personal information. In them:

  • there are no accounts, sign-ups or logins;
  • we never ask for a child's name, age, email address, photo, voice or location;
  • there is no advertising of any kind, and no third-party analytics or tracking;
  • there are no chat or social features;
  • any purchases or links that leave the app are placed behind a parental gate.

We build our children's apps to follow the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the GDPR, the Apple App Store Kids Category guidelines and the Google Play Families Policy. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under the age of digital consent in your country). If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time what personal information we hold about you (or your child), and ask us to correct or delete it. Depending on where you live (for example, in the EU, UK or California) you may have further rights under local law. Email hello@hatchlabsllc.com and we will reply.

Keeping information safe

We use reasonable measures to protect information and keep it only as long as needed for the purpose it was collected for, or as the law requires.

This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking. Our hosting provider may keep standard technical logs (such as IP addresses) for security and reliability, under its own privacy policy.

App stores

Our apps are downloaded through the Apple App Store and Google Play. Apple and Google may collect information about downloads, purchases and devices under their own privacy policies, which we do not control.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle information, we will update this page and the date above before the change takes effect.
